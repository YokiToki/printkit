# Design

## Context

`SourceParams = {kind: 'distance' | 'brightness', contrast, gamma, invert, smooth, despeckle}`. In `buildGrid` the brightness source samples the gray plane at the grid nodes inside the silhouette and maps it with `brightnessValue` to heights `floor + value × (hmax − floor)`; then the rim ceiling, smoothing (masked Gaussian), profile (bevel, dome, terrace, flat, engraved), detail lines and patterns. `buildSilhouette` makes the mask from brightness or alpha; `33` adds the AI matte and its registry; `32` the ML runtime and model manager.

## What the PoC showed (cat photo)

Depth V2 Small gives a smooth depth map (the owner found it good as a form); on its own it does not carry the facial features. Local contrast of the brightness on a face crop (400 × 300 px of a 960 × 1280 photo) makes the eyes, nose, whiskers, stripes and ear clear, and 4 tone levels keep the face recognisable; on the whole frame the face is too small (`poc/ai/findings/`). The contour bevel is the existing form. The three sources therefore play different roles, and the PoC combiner is the tool to pick the mix.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- A recognisable portrait relief from a photo by mixing contour, depth and detail, with controls the user understands; nothing changes for existing keychains.
- Everything stays in the browser.

**Non-Goals:**
- Face detection, sculpting, line extraction, colour.

## Decisions

- **One mix, three shares.** In `buildGrid`, with `source.kind = 'photo'`, the height value at a node inside the silhouette is
  `value = (wC·contour + wD·depth + wT·detail) / (wC + wD + wT)`, all three terms in 0…1, then the existing contrast, gamma, invert and smoothing, then `floor + value × (hmax − floor)`, then the rim ceiling, profile and patterns as for any source. `contour` is the existing bevel value (`reliefValue` of the distance to the edge, so wC alone equals the Distance source), `depth` the depth plane normalised inside the silhouette, `detail` the boosted brightness. A share is stored in 0…100 and normalised by the sum, so the user sets proportions and the relief always uses the full height range; sum zero falls back to Contour. Shares of unavailable sources (no depth computed yet) count as zero and the panel says so.
- **Why normalised shares and not an additive detail.** Three sliders that always add up to the whole are easy to read ("40 % depth, 30 % detail") and cannot overflow the range. Detail is zero-centred (mean 0.5), so raising its share changes structure, not the mean height. The bevel ceiling keeps the edge rolling off, so a high detail share never produces a cliff at the edge.
- **Depth plane like the matte of `33`.** The main thread runs the depth model through the `32` client and gets a plane at the image's resolution, normalised 0…1 (2nd–98th percentile; farther 0, nearer 1), registered once in the worker (`registerDepth`, `depthRegistry`); params carry `source.depthId`. In `buildGrid` it is sampled like brightness and renormalised over the silhouette pixels (robust min/max inside the mask) so a far background does not waste the range. Sliders only rebuild in the worker.
- **Depth model and input.** Depth Anything V2 Small ONNX (`onnx-community/depth-anything-v2-small-ONNX`, Apache-2.0): q4f16 20 MB, fp16 50 MB, weights in a separate `.onnx_data` file, so the runtime needs external-data support (path, bytes and SHA-256 of both files in the registry entry). Registry kind `depth` next to `matte`; the manager shows the kind and the picker filters by it. Input: the working image (the crop when set, long side capped at 2048), resized to a multiple of 14 with the aspect kept (518 for this model), output resampled bilinearly back. Depth Anything 3 Small (Apache-2.0, newer) is compared in the spike; Base and Large are non-commercial and not offered.
- **Depth and the crop.** Depth computed on the crop has more pixels on the face. The depth record stores the crop it was computed for; when the crop changes the depth is kept and resampled (stale but usable) and marked "computed for another crop, recompute for better detail"; the user decides to re-run (a few seconds). Contour and Detail follow the crop live.
- **Detail boost.** `localContrast(gray, w, h, radiusPx, amount)`: subtract a Gaussian-blurred copy, divide by the local standard deviation (blurred squared deviation with a floor against noise), `0.5 + 0.5·tanh(amount·v)`. The radius is stored in **mm of the part** (`boostRadius`, default ≈ 1.4 mm, found with the PoC at about 14 px on a 400 px face crop printed 40 mm wide) and converted to pixels from the crop and the keychain size, so the same value looks the same at any resolution. Amount 0 = off (detail is then a flat 0.5, the same as no detail).
- **Tone levels.** `levels` 0 = continuous (default), 2–8 = quantise the detail value, with `levelSoftness` (0 hard steps, 1 smooth ramps) so the steps are printable slopes; only the detail term is quantised, so contour and depth stay smooth. The Terraced profile still works on the final height.
- **Crop.** `crop = {x, y, w, h}` fractions of the image (default full). `buildSilhouette` and `buildGrid` read the planes (gray, alpha, matte, depth) through a cropped view made once per change in the worker; size, text and mount fractions stay relative to the silhouette box. `CropEditor` over the image: drag, corner and edge handles, aspect lock (free, 1:1, 4:3, 3:4), fit to subject from the AI mask box, reset; minimum 5 % of a side; live update of the preview; also in the enlarged mask dialog. Changing the crop never re-runs the matte model.
- **Whole frame silhouette.** `silhouette.source: 'frame'`: every pixel of the crop is material; threshold, close, areas and specks are hidden. With the Base plate (rounded rectangle, circle) this is a portrait plaque; the relief edge still rolls off through the bevel ceiling and rim.
- **Outline without steps.** Stairs on the edge come from three places, and each is removed: (1) a binary mask quantises the edge to a pixel, so the outline is taken from a continuous field instead (the soft matte of `33`, the grey level for Brightness, a blurred mask otherwise, iso-line at 0.5, sub-pixel); (the smoothing is the one that already works for cutouts: `traceSilhouette` blurs the mask with σ = 1 px at 4 px/mm, that is about 0.25 mm, takes the iso-line at 128 and simplifies it by 0.15 mm; the outline here reuses that routine or its parameters, expressed in mm of the part and exposed as an "Outline smoothness" value, default 0.25 mm, so thin spikes can keep sharper tips with a smaller value); (2) cutting the relief by whole grid cells leaves a staircase of cells, so the outline is traced as a vector polygon (`traceAlpha`, marching squares with sub-pixel interpolation, as for cutouts and text), simplified with a small tolerance (0.03–0.05 mm) and smoothed (Chaikin or a curvature-limited smoothing that keeps real corners such as spike tips), and the relief is clipped by that polygon exactly as the Shape mode already does with `shapeCrossSection` (extrude the polygon as a `CrossSection`, intersect with the relief, so the wall is exact and independent of the grid cell); (3) the grid only supplies heights, so its cell size no longer limits the outline. The PoC shows the effect with marching-squares clipping of the grid cells along the iso-line (`poc/ai/keychain.ts`): the outline is smooth to well under the cell size. Large enclosed areas stay open and small ones between strokes are filled through the existing areas of change 18 (`findHoles`), not a new rule.
- **Presets.** Subtle / Portrait / Deep set the three shares, `hmax − floor`, boost radius and amount and levels (Portrait: about 30 / 40 / 30, 2 mm range, radius 1.4 mm, 4 soft levels; to be confirmed with the spike) and show Custom when edited; data next to the profile presets.
- **Heights and printing.** A height field grows from a flat back (existing design), prints face up without supports. Detail finer than the grid cell is lost: `quality` and the resolution warnings apply, with a hint when the cell is larger than about a third of the boost radius.
- **Persistence and migration.** Depth planes are stored in `imageStore` as `depth` records keyed by image, model, revision and crop, restored on load, kept when the model is deleted, dropped with the image, memory fallback with the `persistent` warning. `crop`, mix, boost and levels are keychain params (`migrateKeychain` fills defaults: full crop, `kind` unchanged, boost 0, levels 0). If the stored depth is missing the Depth share counts as zero with a "run again" message, never an empty relief.
- **Availability.** The Photo source appears only in Image mode, not for SVG images, and Depth only when a depth model exists; Contour and Detail work without any model.
- **UI.** Relief source: Distance | Brightness | Photo. Under Photo: three share sliders with the mix as a stacked bar, the model picker and Compute depth (progress, cancel, "for another crop" note), Detail boost (amount, radius as `LengthField`), Tone levels (count, softness), presets, the existing contrast, gamma, invert and smoothing, and a height greyscale view next to the mask preview. `CropEditor` in the Image section; the Silhouette source gains "Whole frame". Strings in en and ru.
- **Performance.** The depth run is a few seconds and cancellable (latest wins); everything else is arithmetic in the worker.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Depth is soft or wrong at fur and at the subject's edge] → the mask clips the edge, the bevel ceiling hides the cliff, Detail adds the fine structure; the shares let the user lean on whichever source works for the photo.
- [Local contrast amplifies noise and JPEG artefacts] → floor in the divisor, despeckle and smoothing, the radius control.
- [A face is too small in the part to read] → the crop, the hint and the quality warning; a keychain-sized part cannot carry hair, the portrait is about eyes, nose and stripes.
- [A second model on top of the matte model] → shared manager and consent of `32`; both small, results are stored.
- [External-data ONNX needs runtime support] → registry and runtime tasks of this change (tested), PoC already loads it.
- [Quantised levels make jagged edges] → softness and the existing smoothing; hard steps are a choice.
- [Three shares are many controls] → presets first; the shares collapse into one stacked bar; a single "Photo" source keeps the panel short.
